## So what — strategic implications

- **China's robotics market is entering the institutional-capital phase.** Unitree's STAR Market IPO (Aug 19) + Dexmal's $3B round in play (Aug 21) + WRC 2026 as the anchor showcase: this is the same arc EV took a decade ago. Chinese humanoid companies are now valued alongside US counterparts at double-digit billions. Expect accelerating export competition.

- **Hot Chips 2026 is the highest-risk 48 hours for Intel's edge-robotics positioning.** NVIDIA (Cosmos 3 Edge on Jetson Thor T3000 already announced July), AMD, and potentially Qualcomm / Google TPU will be on stage at Stanford. Any new edge-inference roadmap beats that narrows the Intel Core Ultra / OpenVINO value proposition warrants immediate tracking.

- **FCC robotics import ban creates a US-market moat — but raises BOM cost.** Foreign-produced humanoids, AMRs, and quadrupeds can no longer get US equipment authorization for new models. This structurally advantages US-designed platforms (Boston Dynamics, Figure, Agility) and increases integration complexity for any buyer exploring Chinese hardware. Watch for conditional-approval applications and workaround announcements in the next 30 days.
