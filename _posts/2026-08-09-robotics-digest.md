## 7. Policy / Standards / Safety

- **FCC Covered List — Foreign Robots** (effective July 28, ongoing enforcement): All new foreign-produced "advanced robotic devices" (autonomous, mobile, sensor-enabled, networked, >2 kg) added to the FCC Covered List, requiring equipment authorization that is no longer available for new foreign-origin products. Scope: humanoids, quadrupeds, wheeled mobile robots. Exemptions: fixed/stationary robotic arms, FDA 510(k)-cleared medical devices, models with prior FCC authorization. National security rationale: surveillance data collection and remote-control vulnerability risk. China has formally protested. Coverage: [IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/fcc-covered-list-mobile-robots) *(date via search index)*, [K&L Gates analysis](https://www.klgates.com/thought-leadership/FCC-Adds-Foreign-Produced-Advanced-Robotic-Devices-to-the-Covered-List-Five-Things-to-Know-8-3-2026), [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/08/03/1141056/trumps-ai-protectionism-has-come-for-robotics/)

## So What — Strategic Implications

1. **FCC ban reshapes US humanoid supply chain overnight.** Chinese robots are locked out of new US sales. This opens white space for US/EU/Korean platforms (Figure, Agility, Boston Dynamics, Agile Robots) and raises costs for US integrators that relied on Chinese hardware. Intel's US-manufactured edge compute story — Core Ultra, NPU, Arc — gets a structural tailwind if positioned with domestic robot OEMs before they lock in supply chains.

2. **Unitree IPO at $9B normalizes humanoid investing for public markets.** A *profitable* humanoid maker (~60% gross margins) going public will catalyze IPO filings from Figure, Apptronik, and 1X. Valuations will re-price. Watch for this to accelerate enterprise customer commitments as buyers prefer listed-company suppliers for durability.

3. **BYD entering humanoids = automotive-scale BOM threat.** Xiao Di leverages EV-derived motors, batteries, and AI — if BYD can deploy at automotive volumes, its per-unit manufacturing cost could undercut pure-play humanoid startups by 40–60%. The FCC ban buys US players time in the domestic market, but BYD will move aggressively into Asia, EU, and LatAm. Watch BOM pricing signals from their next disclosure.

4. **Hot Chips (Aug 23–25) is the next critical inflection.** The three weeks between now and HC38 are the calm before the storm. Any Jetson Thor 2 hint, Intel NPU roadmap update, or new edge-AI accelerator disclosure will land there — and will immediately reprice the Intel-vs-NVIDIA competitive picture for on-robot compute. Prioritize coverage of those sessions.
