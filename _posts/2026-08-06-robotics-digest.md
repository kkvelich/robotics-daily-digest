## So what — strategic implications

1. **Unitree's offer price today is the market's first hard public anchor for humanoid valuation.** At ¥40B vs. Figure AI's $39B (zero revenue), the gap between profitable Chinese scale and loss-making Western aspiration is now quantified. Watch Aug 10 oversubscription ratio as the clearest demand signal of the year.

2. **The autonomous fleet stack is bifurcating.** Moove's $250M at $2.1B shows that operating the fleet (depot infrastructure, charging "Nests," fleet management) is becoming a distinct, investable layer separate from the AV technology itself (Waymo). This pattern will reach humanoid logistics: expect "robot-ops" infrastructure plays modeled on Moove to emerge for warehouse humanoids.

3. **Two-tier deal market visible today:** $250M infrastructure rounds vs. $6.5M dexterity-data seed. Capital is concentrating at the infrastructure layer AND at the earliest embodied-AI bets — mid-range humanoid Series A/B is still funding-sparse. Implications for Intel: infrastructure (Moove-style) uses commodity compute; early dexterity startups (Avatar) are the design-win target before scale locks in GPU/NPU preferences.

4. **Hot Chips (Aug 23–25) is Intel's next real window.** No silicon signal moved today. With Jetson T3000 already shipping samples to OEMs and Qualcomm DragonWing IQ10 positioned as the power-efficient alternative, the Core Ultra Series 3 / NPU roadmap needs a visible update before the Q4 design-cycle freeze.
