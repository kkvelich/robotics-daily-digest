## So what — strategic implications

- **Unitree's IPO demand is a market temperature gauge, not just a company event.** 8,289× retail oversubscription at 219× earnings is historically extreme — comparable in enthusiasm to early Chinese EV battery stocks. Institutional and retail capital is clearly pricing humanoid robotics as a multi-decade platform. Expect Figure AI, Agility, Apptronik, and 1X to price any future equity events (IPO or late-stage private) significantly higher on the back of this signal.
- **Robo Inc. confirms a durable US business model: compliance wrapper around Chinese hardware.** Until US-native humanoids achieve price/performance parity with Unitree, enterprises will pay a margin for integration and security hardening rather than wait. This undercuts the urgency for US-first OEM procurement — a real headwind for Figure 03, Digit, and Apollo in near-term commercial deals.
- **Intel's edge-robotics position has no new wins today.** The dominant near-term silicon narrative remains NVIDIA's Jetson T3000/T2000 Blackwell mid-range expansion (announced July 15, Q1 2027 availability) paired with Cosmos 3 Edge — a world foundation model bundled into the platform. OpenVINO is absent from recent deployment announcements. The Microchip/Hailo deal (signed July 24, closing by Sep 30) will consolidate a competing edge-inference stack. Intel needs a visible win at a named customer or a Jetson-competitive product announcement to change the narrative.
- **Watch this week:** Unitree share payment deadline (Aug 12) and expected listing date; any BYD humanoid specification disclosure (debut occurred in early August at Di Space showrooms, specs still withheld); Hot Chips 2026 abstract releases as the conference approaches Aug 23.
