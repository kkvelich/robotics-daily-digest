## So what — strategic implications

- **Unitree's IPO reprices the whole sector.** A ¥150.80/share print (45% above forecast) signals institutional conviction in Chinese humanoid makers — and sets a public comp table. Watch how this pressure-tests Western private valuations (Figure AI at $39B no-revenue, 1X, Apptronik).
- **The FCC Covered-List move is the most consequential near-term policy action.** It cuts off US market access for Unitree, AgiBot, BYD Xiao Di and any Chinese robot with radio hardware. Chinese makers will redirect export focus to EU, SE Asia, and Middle East faster than planned.
- **NVIDIA's compute stack is pulling away.** Cosmos 3 Edge + Jetson T3000/T2000 are the default physical-AI substrate for 2026 robot programs. Intel's hybrid-edge story (Core Ultra + OpenVINO Physical AI) needs a major customer win before year-end to remain credible as a platform alternative — not just a cost-down option.
- **Watch WRC 2026 (Aug 19–23) closely.** 150+ Chinese product debuts in a single week. New silicon partnerships (Horizon Robotics, Cambricon, Rockchip) and production-scale commitments from AgiBot / XPeng IRON are the items most likely to reshape the competitive map.
