## So what — strategic implications

- **Unitree's stock debut (Aug 17–21) is a market-sentiment test.** A strong pop validates the "humanoid revenue" thesis and will accelerate VC check-writing into the sector globally. A flat or negative open would be the first signal that public markets are more skeptical than private backers.

- **WRC Release Day (Aug 19) will likely flood the China section next week.** With 150+ first-ever products announced in a single day, filter signal from noise: watch for compute platform disclosures (Horizon Journey 7, Black Sesame A2000, Cambricon MLU590) and deployment contract announcements, not form-factor demos.

- **Hot Chips is the key silicon-watch moment of Q3.** If NVIDIA's Rubin/LPU session includes robotics inference benchmarks, or if any hyperscaler silicon shows edge-deployable profiles, it reconfigures the hybrid-edge competitive picture. Intel has no confirmed Hot Chips slot as of this writing — absence at this venue would itself be a signal.

- **Intel hybrid-edge opportunity window:** Core Ultra Series 3 (180 TOPS, industrial-certified, in-market) is well-positioned for the pre-WRC design-win cycle. The NVIDIA T3000/T2000 modules don't ship until Q1 2027. This 6-month gap is the moment to close design wins with OEMs who need to spec their 2027-model platforms now.
