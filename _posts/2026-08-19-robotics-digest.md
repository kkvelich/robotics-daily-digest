## So What — Strategic Implications

- **Unitree's $50B public valuation changes the game.** It's now the reference price for the entire humanoid sector. Every future funding round or IPO — US or Chinese — will benchmark against it. At >40% revenue overseas, Unitree is a global commercial force despite the FCC ban on new imports.

- **WRC's "Release Day" = the real product clock.** 150 world debuts today from Chinese firms signal that the pilot-to-production transition is happening faster in China than elsewhere. The commercial test — sustained factory throughput, not demos — is what the next four days will reveal.

- **Intel's edge-AI position in humanoids is under compounding pressure.** The publicly traded, globally recognized humanoid platform (Unitree) uses Jetson Thor for its reference compute. Xiaomi's debut adds another Tier-1 consumer brand to the humanoid field without any disclosed Intel silicon. The hybrid-edge narrative needs a design win in a named humanoid product — urgently.

- **Watch Procurement Day (Aug 20) at WRC.** Named customer commitments from Chinese manufacturers — auto OEMs, electronics assemblers — will be the clearest near-term signal of whether humanoid economics have crossed the deployment threshold.
