## 7. Policy / standards / safety

- **⚠️ FCC bans Chinese humanoid + quadruped robot imports (US)** — FCC rules announced July 28–29 bar US imports of *new* foreign-made humanoid robots, quadruped robots ("robot dogs"), and connected power inverters (renewable/battery/data-center grid links). Existing deployed units are exempt. Rationale: remote-hijacking and data-exfiltration risk from always-connected robotics; supply-chain sovereignty. Driven by White House interagency national security panel. China's ~85% market share means near-term US supply shock for buyers not yet contracted with domestic producers. [UPI Jul 29](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/World-News/2026/07/29/bans-Chinese-humanoid-robots/4671785312882/) (date via search index) · [WaPo Jul 28](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/07/28/fcc-bans-foreign-humanoid-robots-expanding-campaign-against-chinese-tech/) (date via search index) · [ABC News Jul 29](https://abcnews.com/Business/wireStory/us-bans-foreign-made-humanoid-robots-targeting-china-135179676) (date via search index)

- **EU AI Act — T-4 days**: Core obligations apply to most EU-market AI systems on **August 2, 2026**, including AI-enabled robots. Robotics OEMs with EU sales must comply under the combined AI Act + Machinery Regulation framework. Not a news event today; flagged as imminent deadline. ## So what — strategic implications

1. **FCC ban creates a domestic humanoid supply vacuum overnight.** With ~85% of global production in China, US buyers face near-term shortage until domestic producers (Agility, Figure, Boston Dynamics, 1X) scale. NVIDIA — the compute partner of record for Agility's Digit — is the clearest near-term silicon winner. Intel needs a credible humanoid reference design now, not in 2027.

2. **Agility's Nasdaq path validates the RaaS model.** $300M in booked multi-year orders at $2.5B valuation (~8× ARR) is aggressive but credible for a market just entering production ramp. The NVIDIA IGX Thor + Halos OS stack is becoming the de facto US humanoid compute standard.

3. **China will redirect capacity to non-US markets aggressively.** Europe, Southeast Asia, and LatAm will see Unitree, AgiBot, and Xpeng Iron at competitive prices. European OEMs face pricing pressure while simultaneously absorbing EU AI Act compliance costs — watch for consolidation.

4. **Watch this week**: Implementation timeline for the FCC ban (rule effective date, enforcement mechanism); whether Congress advances the Schumer-Cotton American Security Robotics Act (government-use ban); any Chinese retaliatory measures targeting US semiconductor or robotics supply chains.
