## So What — Strategic Implications

1. **Unitree's 2,760x oversubscription resets the humanoid market's public benchmark.** A profitable manufacturer shipping at scale is now priced in public markets at ~$9B. This creates daily mark-to-market pressure on Figure ($39B, no revenue), Apptronik, and 1X. Expect a round of enterprise buyers to demand demonstrated unit economics before committing large deployment contracts — good for operators like Agility/GXO who have production data, challenging for pre-deployment startups.

2. **Intel's $15B raise is the largest capital commitment to "physical AI" by a chip company this year.** The explicit use of "physical AI" in Intel's prospectus language is new and deliberate — it tells the market that edge-robotics compute is now a named P&L line. Capital goes to 14A (NPU efficiency) and foundry (capacity for robotics silicon customers). Paired with Hot Chips in 13 days, this sets up a potential moment where Intel shows both the money and the roadmap. Watch for correlation between HC38 announcements and Intel's stock recovery.

3. **DeepSeek + Unitree = the China-native full-stack is taking shape.** Yesterday it was a strategic investment; today Unitree's public listing locks it in as the structural core of China's humanoid ecosystem. A liquid, publicly traded hardware platform with a sovereign AI software partner creates a supply chain that is insulated from US export-control pressure at every layer — compute, model, and manufacturing.

4. **Tomorrow's watch:** Intel offering priced and bookbuilt (watch for investor mix — strategic vs. financial); Unitree payment deadline Aug 12; any Q2 earnings from robotics-adjacent companies that confirm or deny physical AI revenue traction.
