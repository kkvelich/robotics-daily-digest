## So what — strategic implications

1. **Unitree's IPO reprices the whole humanoid market.** At ¥42B floor with real profit, it makes loss-making Western peers look overvalued — or it validates that robotics multiples can hold. Subscription oversubscription on Aug 10 will be the clearest demand signal we've had in 2026.

2. **BYD's Xiao Di entry is a training-data flywheel, not just a product launch.** 6,000+ dealerships = massive real-world interaction data at zero incremental cost. Watch BYD's compute choices carefully — they have the balance sheet and supply chain to become an OEM-style silicon buyer at scale.

3. **Family office capital into robotics broadens and extends the funding cycle.** Arnault/Bezos/Premji entering signals the asset class has crossed from "venture bet" to "family-office-grade thesis." This sustains funding depth and reduces the urgency for exits, meaning fewer fire-sale consolidation events.

4. **Hot Chips (Aug 23-25) is Intel's next scheduled opportunity.** With NVIDIA Jetson T3000/T2000 now the design-win target for Q1'27 robots, Intel needs a credible Panther Lake / NPU roadmap signal before OEM design cycles lock. Two weeks out.
